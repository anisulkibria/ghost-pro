01

The parts you would
rather not run yourself.

A Ghost install is a server, a certificate, a mail route, a backup job and an upgrade path. All of it is ours.

Your own domain

Point a CNAME at us. The certificate is issued and renewed on its own, and www and the apex both resolve.

Backups you can take

Taken on a schedule and kept off-box — and you can pull one yourself from the dashboard, whenever you like.

Upgrades, handled

New Ghost releases are rolled out for you. Your theme and your content are untouched; nothing asks you to run a command.

Bring your own Mailgun

Newsletters send through your own Mailgun account, at Mailgun's prices. No markup from us, no send quota, and the sending reputation is yours to keep.

Members without a meter

No ceiling on your list, on any plan — we do not charge per reader. Paid tiers run through your own Stripe account, and the numbers that matter are built in.

Still your Ghost

Custom themes, the Admin and Content APIs, webhooks and integrations. Nothing is locked down, and your content exports whole.

02

One process per
publication.

Most hosts put a hundred sites in one application and hope. Here, your publication is its own operating-system process, with its own database and its own memory, supervised on its own and restarted on its own.

A neighbour's traffic spike is a neighbour's problem. An upgrade is rolled one site at a time. And because the edge proxy only routes, a site that is asleep costs nothing and wakes on the first request.
