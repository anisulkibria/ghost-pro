How it runs

One process per
publication.

Most hosts put a hundred sites in one application and hope. Here, your publication is its own operating-system process, with its own database and its own memory, supervised on its own and restarted on its own.

A neighbour's traffic spike is a neighbour's problem. An upgrade is rolled one site at a time. And because the edge proxy only routes, a site that is asleep costs nothing and wakes on the first request.

Questions

Fair questions.

The things people ask before they move a publication.

Do you take a cut of my membership revenue?

No. Paid tiers are billed through your own Stripe account, so what your readers pay goes to you. We charge for the plan and nothing per reader.

How does a custom domain work?

Point a CNAME at us. The certificate is issued and renewed on its own, and both www and the apex resolve. Starter includes one custom domain, Creator two and Business ten.

What counts toward the email allowance?

Each newsletter recipient and each transactional message counts as one email. The allowance resets every month, including on yearly plans, and usage is tracked so a send never surprises you.

Is anything about Vuuut locked down?

No. Every plan includes custom themes, the Admin and Content APIs, webhooks and custom integrations.

What does one process per publication change for me?

Your site has its own process, database and memory. A neighbour's traffic spike stays a neighbour's problem, and upgrades are rolled out one site at a time rather than to everybody at once.

Can I leave?

Whenever you like. Cancel anytime, your content exports whole, and you can pull a backup yourself from the dashboard.
